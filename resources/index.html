Tracking and reminders

These answers explain FA Tracker features. For a question about your own application, contact support.

Ask a question for free
What do status-change alerts tell me?

FA Tracker checks tracked applications for updates and can send you a notification when it detects a change. Turn on notifications on your device to receive alerts.

Can community data predict my wait?

Community insights describe historical timings from FA Tracker users. They are independent of SAPS and do not predict the date or outcome of an individual application.

How do renewal reminders work?

Record an expiry date in the app and enable notifications to receive renewal reminders. Keep your recorded dates up to date.
