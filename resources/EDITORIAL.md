# FA Tracker resource publishing

The live-ready index contains product FAQs on status alerts, community data and renewal reminders. Place future reviewed articles under `resources/<slug>/index.html` and use the Resources page as the shared template. Group articles by applications and status, competency and renewals, records and estates, and sources and administrative procedures. Use real link text and link to two or three closely related articles at the end of each page. Add search when the library becomes large enough that browsing by topic no longer works.

For each article record a title, slug, topic, summary, author or reviewer, primary sources, verification date, review-by date, related URLs, and owner-approved revision. Link to the primary source alongside procedural claims. Keep drafts and outdated articles out of the deployment tree and sitemap until reviewed. Check captioning, alt text and ownership for videos and infographics. The existing marketing website-knowledge-hub R01 catalogue and approval checks are the editorial inputs. Its draft articles were excluded from this preview.

## Adding a series

Once at least one article in a series is approved, give the series a heading on the Resources index. Show its topic and a short summary, then link to the approved articles in reading order. For a video or infographic, include a text summary and an accessible transcript or equivalent beside the media. The Resources URL and existing FAQs can remain in place as the library grows.
